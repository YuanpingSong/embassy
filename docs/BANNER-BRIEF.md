# Embassy — GitHub Promo Banner Brief

You are designing the GitHub social-preview / promotional banner for
**Embassy**, an open-source local gateway that lets a user's AI coding
agents (Claude Code sessions and Codex desktop tasks) send messages to each
other on one machine. Its metaphor: an embassy — a warm, neutral meeting
place where different powers talk under agreed rules.

## Deliverable

- 1280×640 PNG (GitHub social preview). Keep every critical element inside
  a centered ~1200×560 safe area; the card gets cropped and rounded on
  X/Slack/iMessage. It must read at thumbnail size (~256px wide).
- Optimized weight (<600 KB preferred).
- Include the wordmark **Embassy** and tagline **A local embassy for your
  AI agents** — legible at thumbnail, harmonized with the art, not pasted-on.

## The brand world (current, user-approved)

Warm anime / Ghibli-adjacent softness. Golden, inviting, high charm. The
existing shipped banner (see `assets/social-preview-anime-c.png` for the
untexted source and `assets/social-preview.png` for the shipped composite)
sets the canon: **three chibi agent mascots collaborating under a big warm
tent**:

- a coral/terracotta creature with a soft sun/starburst motif,
- a clean teal-and-white round robot,
- a deep-blue whale.

They pass envelopes and scrolls between laptops — messages are the product.
The tent (also the favicon mark, `assets/mark-seal.svg`) is the embassy.
You may evolve the composition, characters' poses, lighting, and framing
freely; keep the cast of three, the tent, and the envelope/message motif.

## Hard constraints

- NO vendor logos, logo-shapes, or brand names (the characters are
  *evocative* of Claude/OpenAI/DeepSeek by palette and vibe only — never
  reproduce a trademark or write a vendor's name).
- No dark/techy ground: warm creams, ambers, corals. No circuit imagery,
  no code rain, no glowing HUDs.
- The tone never overclaims: it's a cozy meeting place, not a sci-fi
  super-intelligence. Charm over spectacle.
- Wordmark: display serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino family), warm ink
  brown (#6b4a26 works on cream); tagline in letterspaced sans, muted
  (#a08054 family).

## Quality bar

Squint test at thumbnail: tent silhouette + three distinct character
colors + wordmark must all survive. If any element vanishes at 256px,
simplify until it doesn't. Deliver 2–3 compositions to choose from.

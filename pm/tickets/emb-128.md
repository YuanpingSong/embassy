## Binding

service.ts ~802–804 and ~841–843 route every non-Claude route incl. registrationMode federated_peer into the LOCAL advertisement path; claude-helper.ts ~80–81 refuses any alias not ending in @<localHost>, so every mirrored route forks a helper that fails (INVALID_CODEX_PEER_ALIAS → NATIVE_ADVERTISEMENT_FAILED) on every catalog reconcile and after every boot. Deliverable: skip advertisement (and unadvertisement) for federated_peer routes; docs say mirrors are not advertised (emb-111 already words it that way). Characterize first: a federated mirror row from a fake catalog → zero helper forks and zero NATIVE_ADVERTISEMENT_FAILED alerts (red on the parent). R3 (helper/process boundary): light gate + adversarial; helper/supervisor + federation tests.
